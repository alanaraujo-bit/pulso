# Code of Conduct

Pulso follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level, background or language.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks, in any space of the project.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/alanaraujo-bit/pulso/security/advisories/new)
titled "Conduct report"; only maintainers can see it.
